# Operator Workspace

## User outcome

An authorized operator can open a workspace, inspect the information available to them and understand the next permitted action.

## Scope

- Entry into the workspace.
- A clear overview of available information.
- Visible loading, empty and error states.

## Acceptance

The workspace shows only authorized content. A failed load cannot appear as an empty successful result. The operator can understand whether to retry, choose another item or request assistance.
